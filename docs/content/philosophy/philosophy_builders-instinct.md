## The Through-Line

"I'm a builder. The medium changes. The instinct doesn't."

From architecture school to a 3D internet city to a $250M superyacht to 80 museum exhibits to AI agent systems for fabrication coordination — each chapter is a different medium, the same underlying drive: take the best available tools, figure out how they serve the craft, and build something that wasn't possible before.

---

## On AI (paraphrased from source)

"I've been leaning into AI since October 2024. Same instinct as every other tool transition in my career — okay, what's possible, how does this serve the craft, how do we make it so the craftsman isn't lost but gets better tools to express themselves."

This is not the posture of someone who adopted a new tool when it became fashionable. It is the posture of someone who has been at the leading edge of each tool cycle — and knows the difference between a tool and the judgment that makes it useful.

---

## On the Role of AI in Fabrication

AI handles the patterns inside the project. The human job is to bring patterns from *outside* it.

AI is incredible at finding patterns inside a domain. What it cannot yet do is recognize that the problem in front of you is structurally identical to one solved ten years ago in a completely different industry. That recognition — and the decision it enables — is where the real value lives.
