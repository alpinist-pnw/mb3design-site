## Master Fabrication

MB3 Design bridges computational design and physical fabrication. Mark Buchanan has been a carpenter since he was fifteen — the craft foundation predates the computational tools and grounds every digital decision in physical reality.

**Fabrication methods regularly applied:**

- CNC router (wood, composite panels — complex organic profiles, unique-per-part)
- Waterjet cutting (steel plate up to 3/4", organic profiles, structural components)
- Laser cutting (sheet steel, thin plate, surface engraving)
- Brake forming (sheet metal components)
- Sand casting (aluminum — demonstrated on Intuit Dome)
- 3D printing at scale (demonstrated on LAX Metro origami sculpture — 3D printed, resin infused)

**Material range:** Structural steel, stainless steel, aluminum (cast and sheet), plywood (specialty faced), wood veneers, composite materials

**Quality standard:** Museum-grade throughout. Structures that thousands of children climb on daily, at National Park Service historic sites, designed by Pritzker-adjacent architecture firms. The quality bar is set by the client type, not by what's "normal" for the fabrication budget.

**Scale:** Single components (36+ unique parts in a single tree sculpture) to full project environments (500+ CNC-carved slices, 564 pages of shop drawings, 3 buildings).
