## Museum-Grade Quality Control

The quality standard for MB3 work is determined by the environment the work lives in: historic buildings on National Park Service land, managed by major cultural institutions, where thousands of visitors interact with the work daily.

**What "museum-grade" means in fabrication:**

- Structural integrity for daily high-traffic use by the public, including children
- Safety margins that are calculated, documented, and traceable back to the 3D model
- Part numbering and traceability: every component has a part number, etched directly on the piece where possible, traceable through the BOM to the 3D model and drawings
- Zero-tolerance on reversibility requirements: if the contract says no permanent alterations to the building, the design is engineered to that constraint from the first model pass
- Installation quality that matches the design quality — the model is not aspirational, it is the standard the installed work is measured against

**The verification chain:** 3D model → shop drawings → CNC output → fabricated parts with part numbers → BOM-verified assembly → installation against as-built scan data

**Demonstrated standard:** Bay Area Discovery Museum production approval signed M. Buchanan, 03/18/2020. Pacific Studio, Director of Technical Design.
