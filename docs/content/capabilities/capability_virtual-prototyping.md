## Virtual Prototyping

MB3 builds the complete exhibit, sculpture, or installation in 3D before a single piece is cut or welded. This is not presentation modeling — it is fabrication-grade modeling, where every component has a part number, every connection to the building is resolved, and every tolerance is checked.

**Why it matters:** Digital iterations cost hours. Physical iterations cost thousands of dollars plus schedule impact. An hour in Rhino saves multiples on the shop floor. An hour on the shop floor saves multiples at the job site.

---

## 3D Scan Integration

For projects in existing buildings — especially historic structures — MB3 laser-scans the as-built interior to create a digital envelope. Every ceiling joist, rafter, column, out-of-plumb wall, and window placement is captured.

The exhibit model is then fitted and tolerance-checked against the scan, not against architectural drawings. Architectural drawings of existing buildings cannot be trusted — especially historic buildings where original plans may not exist or may not match what was actually built.

**What this eliminates:** On-site surprises. Structural connections that don't align. Scribing and shimming during installation. Rework caused by field conditions that were never modeled.

**Demonstrated on:** Bay Area Discovery Museum (Fort Baker, Golden Gate National Recreation Area) — entire building interiors across 3 buildings scanned, exhibits modeled against scan data, every tree-to-joist seismic connection resolved before fabrication.

---

## The Proof

At BADM, the install crew specifically noted the amount of up-front thinking in the model. Rework was estimated at 60–80% less than what Pacific Studio normally expects on ambitious installations. Integrated leveling feet eliminated the need to shim and scribe large plywood assemblies on site. Every irregular joist placement was accounted for. The crew walked in and the connections were where they needed to be.

Pacific Studio adopted this approach on virtually all subsequent projects.
