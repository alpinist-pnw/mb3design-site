## Project Overview

**Artist:** Glenn Kaino
**Location:** LAX Metro station
**Title:** Distance of the Sun
**Role:** Project Lead (Code2Matter, 2023–2025)
**Scale:** 150 feet

---

## The Work

A 150-foot origami sculpture scaled 10x from a real origami form. The original was 3D scanned, then scaled up geometrically. The resulting structure was 3D printed, resin infused, and installed at the LAX Metro station. Software: Rhino and SolidWorks.

---

## Technical Notes

The project demonstrates the MB3 pipeline for large-scale organic-form public art: physical artifact → 3D scan → parametric scale-up → fabrication engineering → installation. The organic geometry of folded paper, when scaled 10x and fabricated in structural materials, requires the same computational design discipline as any other complex surface — the form is locked, the challenge is translating it into buildable, installable structure.
