## Project Overview

**Artist:** Glenn Kaino
**Location:** LAX/Metro Transit Center Station
**Role:** Project Lead (Code2Matter, contracted via MB3 Design)
**Year:** Completed 2025
**Scale:** 150 feet — a physical origami form 3D-scanned and scaled 10x, 3D printed, resin infused
