## Project Overview

**Artist:** Glenn Kaino
**Location:** Intuit Dome arena, Inglewood, CA
**Role:** Project Lead for fabrication
**Year:** 2024 (completed)
**Employer at time:** Code2Matter (2023–2025)

---

## The Work

A 60-foot clipper ship sculpture installed inside the Intuit Dome arena. 26 steel pylons with sand-cast aluminum backboard sails.

Mark Buchanan served as project lead for fabrication: material exploration, rapid prototyping, full pipeline from 3D model to truck logistics to crane installation.

---

## The Pipeline

Model → Fabrication → Install — every piece planned before it left the shop.

The assembly was documented and numbered completely in 3D. Truck packing was planned by weight and sequence. On-site installation was crane-driven. The discipline applied here mirrors the Bay Area Discovery Museum methodology at a different scale and material set: front-load the model, coordinate virtually, fabricate with certainty.

---

## Context

This project established MB3's capability in large-scale public art fabrication with complex structural assemblies. It followed the same computational design discipline applied across marine, museum, and manufacturing work — different domain, same pattern.
